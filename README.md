# Monique Rosa Alves

### Information Systems Professional | Prospective PhD Student

**Research Interests:** Human-Centered AI | Multi-Agent Systems | Human-AI Interaction | AI-Supported Decision-Making

## About Me

I am a Brazilian Information Systems professional with over 15 years of experience in Information Technology, particularly in the telecommunications industry.

My professional background includes enterprise systems, systems integration, digital transformation, and the coordination of complex IT projects involving multiple stakeholders.

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, a postgraduate specialization in Information and Communication Technology Management, and an MBA in Project Management from Fundação Getulio Vargas (FGV).

My professional experience has motivated my interest in investigating how intelligent systems can support communication, coordination, and decision-making in complex organizational environments.

## Research Interests

My research interests focus on Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence, Multi-Agent Systems, Human-AI Interaction, and AI-supported decision-making.

I am particularly interested in investigating how collaborative AI agents can integrate information from multiple sources, identify communication gaps, and support coordination and decision-making in complex organizations while maintaining transparency, reliability, and human oversight.
